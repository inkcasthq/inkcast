## Editions (working model)

| Edition | For | Grants beyond the free licence | Typical metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agency** | Agencies, freelancers, firms publishing for clients | **Client publishing** for a set number of client brands, using self-hosted Inkcast or Inkcast Cloud | per client brand or per seat |
| **Service provider** | Businesses that **monetise** Inkcast: publishing or content services sold to others | Providing those services with Inkcast | per seat or per volume |
| **OEM / embedded** | Products and platforms embedding Inkcast features | Embedding and redistributing inside the licensee's product, optionally white-labelled | annual fee and/or revenue share |
| **Hosted / white-label** | Partners offering Inkcast as a service under their brand | Hosting for third parties; white-label rights; trademark licence where agreed | annual fee and/or revenue share |
| **Enterprise** | Any organisation needing assurances | Warranty, IP indemnity, support SLAs, security review, private builds | annual subscription |

Inkcast Cloud (the hosted SaaS operated by ScalarOps) is sold separately, under its own Terms of Service. A subscription includes the rights to use the hosted service for the plan's purposes, including client publishing on Agency plans.

## Terms every commercial licence should cover

- **Licensed use:** edition, permitted uses, number of client brands, seats or volume, and affiliates covered.
- **Territory and term:** territory, term and renewal; what happens to deployments on expiry (wind-down period).
- **Branding:** white-label scope and any trademark licence, with brand guidelines.
- **Source and redistribution:** whether the licensee may distribute binaries, and must keep notices.
- **Support and service:** support and service levels; security and vulnerability notification.
- **Warranty and liability:** warranty, IP indemnity and limitation of liability.
- **Compliance:** audit or usage reporting proportional to the metric; confidentiality.
- **Third-party platforms:** the platforms' own terms (X, Meta, TikTok and others) still apply to the licensee's use of them.
- **Governing law and disputes.**
