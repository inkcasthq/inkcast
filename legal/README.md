## Model at a glance

| Licence | Who it covers |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Own content; self-hosting; modifying; sharing with notices; consultant set-up for one client's own content |
| **Commercial** | Client publishing (agencies); monetising Inkcast-based services; embedding or packaging in products or SaaS; hosting for others; reselling or white-labelling; enterprise assurances |
| **Contributions** | Accepted under the CLA, which keeps the commercial licences possible |

## Open items for counsel

- **Governing law:** set it in both CLAs and the additional permissions.
- **Enforceability:** confirm the agency/client-publishing rule and the monetisation rule hold under PolyForm Shield's competition terms, or add explicit terms.
- **Agreement templates:** commercial master agreement, order form, and Inkcast Cloud Terms of Service.
- **Signing:** CLA Assistant set-up, and signature records retained and exportable.
- **Trademark:** filing for "Inkcast".
- **Ownership:** an IP ownership memo confirming ScalarOps owns all code to date, with no claims from employers or clients.
