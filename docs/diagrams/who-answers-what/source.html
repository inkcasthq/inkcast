Who answers which question

Three systems, three questions, no overlap. Keycloak says who you are, OpenFGA says whether you may act on this object, and the Inkcast database says whether the thing is allowed right now. Keeping them apart is what lets a junior change one without breaking the other two.

One request, three owners
